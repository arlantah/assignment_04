I learned that keeping lineage makes a payroll result easier to trust. The
copy-add-return steps preserve the original text while adding cleaned values that are universal.
I had to think through most carefully was the left merge. The timesheet
and roster have different grains, and using the timesheet as the left frame
ensures a person with no roster match stays in the result with NaN roster data
instead of being silently dropped. The pay label can then flag that row as
unmatched.

I also learned to keep the app focused on assembly: it calls the payroll
pipeline, uses Series.sum() for totals, and presents the result and export
without repeating the cleaning or pay calculations.
 To practice, I will make a small pair of DataFrames with one matching and one unknown employee ID, run the
left merge, and check the rows and NaN values before testing the full pipeline.
